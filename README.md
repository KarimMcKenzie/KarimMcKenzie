# Karim McKenzie

### AI + Research + Program Operations

I build independent operating analyses at the intersection of **AI, scientific research, program management, philanthropy, and complex operations**.

My work focuses on the layer between ambitious ideas and successful execution: how funding, institutions, partnerships, governance, infrastructure, and program operations come together to turn promising opportunities into scalable and durable outcomes.



## Selected Work

### 🧬 [From Scientific Opportunity to Scientific Public Good](https://github.com/KarimMcKenzie/public-data-for-health-operating-framework)

An independent operating framework exploring how philanthropic investment can translate scientifically valuable data into accessible, usable, and durable scientific public goods.

### 🔬 [Academic Research Partnerships & Programs](https://github.com/KarimMcKenzie/academic-research-partnerships)

An independent operating framework exploring how AI capabilities can translate into research partnerships, institutional adoption, and measurable scientific value.

### 🧪 [From API Credits to Scientific Impact](https://github.com/KarimMcKenzie/ai-for-science-partnership-framework)

An independent operating framework exploring how AI for Science partnerships can move from promising research opportunities to evidence of meaningful scientific acceleration.

### ⚙️ [NVIDIA Academic Ecosystem](https://github.com/KarimMcKenzie/nvidia-academic-ecosystem)

An independent case study exploring program strategy and execution across NVIDIA's academic and research ecosystem.
